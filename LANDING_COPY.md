## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial. 
You enter your tools, you get your audit. 
Email is only asked after you see results — 
never before.

**Q: How does the audit work?**
A: You input which AI tools you use, which plan 
you are on, how many seats, and your monthly spend. 
The engine checks whether your plan matches your 
team size and use case, flags overlap between tools, 
and surfaces cheaper alternatives with the same 
capability. The math is shown — not hidden.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Your audit data is stored only if you choose 
to save your report via email. The shareable link 
strips your email and company name — only tool 
names and savings numbers are included in the 
public version.

**Q: Who is Credex and why are they offering this free?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits 
sourced from companies that over-purchased. This 
tool is free because it helps both sides — you find 
out where you are overspending, and for users with 
significant savings opportunities, Credex can often 
provide those same tools at a lower price through 
their credit marketplace.

**Q: What if my tools show no savings?**
A: Then you will see that. We do not manufacture 
savings to make the tool look useful. If your 
current stack is well-optimized, the audit will 
say so — and you can sign up to be notified when 
new optimizations become available for your stack.
