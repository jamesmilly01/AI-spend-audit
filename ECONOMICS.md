# Unit Economics

## What is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assumptions:
- Average deal size: $2,000 (one-time credit purchase)
- Gross margin on resold credits: ~30%
- Gross profit per deal: $600
- Repeat purchase rate: 40% within 6 months
- LTV estimate: $600 + (40% × $600) = $840

**A converted customer is worth ~$840 to Credex.**

With 7 purchases/month from above:
**CAC = $1,000 / 7 = ~$143 per customer**

## What Makes This Profitable

LTV ($840) vs CAC ($143) = **LTV:CAC ratio of 5.9x**

Industry benchmark for healthy SaaS: 3x+
This tool clears that bar comfortably.

Payback period: CAC $143 / ($840 LTV / 12 months) 
= ~2 months payback

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR = ~$833k revenue in 18 months
At $2,000 average deal: **417 purchases needed**
= ~28 purchases/month by month 18

To get 28 purchases/month:
- Need ~9,300 audits/month (at 0.3% audit→purchase)
- Need ~37,000 visitors/month

**Is this achievable?**

Month 1-3: Organic seeding — 2,000 visits/month
Month 4-6: SEO + directory listings kick in — 
           8,000 visits/month
Month 7-12: Referral loop + Product Hunt — 
            20,000 visits/month
Month 13-18: Credex paid distribution + 
             partnerships — 37,000 visits/month

Key assumptions that must hold:
1. Audit completion rate stays above 20%
2. At least 10% of high-savings audits book 
   a consultation
3. Credex closes 25-30% of consultations
4. Average deal size holds at $2,000+
5. Credex email list has 500+ warm contacts 
   to seed early traction

**Biggest risk:** Audit completion rate drops if 
form is too long. Solution: progressive disclosure — 
show results after 2 tools, ask for more later.
